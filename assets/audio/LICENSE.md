# Audio sample licenses

## Drum samples

Files `kick.wav`, `snare.wav`, `hat-closed.wav` and `hat-open.wav` are selected
and renamed samples from `Boochi44/free-drum-samples`, kit `02-bounce`.

The source repository releases all samples under Creative Commons Zero 1.0
(CC0 1.0): https://github.com/Boochi44/free-drum-samples

## Bass samples

Files `bass-c2.wav` and `bass-c3.wav` are selected and renamed short-neck
fretless bass samples from `cleary/samples-flbass`.

The source repository releases the samples under Creative Commons Zero 1.0
(CC0 1.0): https://github.com/cleary/samples-flbass

No attribution is required by CC0. These notices are retained to document the
origin of the bundled offline assets.
