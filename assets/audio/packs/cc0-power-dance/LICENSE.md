# Power Dance CC0 sample library

All audio files in this directory are previews of sounds published on Freesound
under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0 1.0). The source sound ID, title, musical
role, source preview URL and available BPM/key metadata are recorded in
`database/seed/cc0_power_dance_samples.json`.

Canonical source page for each file:

`https://freesound.org/s/<sound-id>/`

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The license and source page of every selected sound were checked individually
on 2026-09-21 before inclusion. The files are stored under their Freesound sound
IDs so that each asset remains traceable to its source record.
