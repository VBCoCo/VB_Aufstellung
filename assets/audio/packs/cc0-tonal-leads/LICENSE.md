The repository contains Freesound's HQ MP3 previews. Piano, bell and flute are mapped to their documented C4. Frequency analysis identifies the two octave-unspecified synth recordings as C3 (about 131 Hz). Tone.Sampler transposes these root notes for generated melodies.
