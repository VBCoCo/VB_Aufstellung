The files remain disabled outside the Super-Admin Power Dance Lab.

## deadrobotmusic – Shibuya family

- Synth source: https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/703690/ (ID 703690)
- Plucks source: https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/703689/ (ID 703689)
- Piano source: https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/703688/ (ID 703688)
- Author: deadrobotmusic
- License: CC0 1.0
- Original tempo/key: 104 BPM, C major
- The bundled OGG files are lossily re-encoded from Freesound's public HQ previews of the respective CC0 sounds.

Tone.js `GrainPlayer` changes playback rate without coupling it to pitch. This lets the Lab
match the constant track BPM while keeping each family's documented key. The optional
register control changes only whole octaves; bass and harmony therefore stay in key.
